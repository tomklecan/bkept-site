Operational Gatekeeper

Vendor Intake Checklist

Data integrity begins at the source. All new vendors must satisfy the following validation points before entry into the financial ecosystem.

01 Compliance Core
Form W-9 (Signed) Must be current (signed within the last 12 months). Mandatory for all service providers.
Certificate of Insurance (COI) Required for all physical trades/contractors. Liability limits must match company policy.
02 Payment Details
Payment Method Intel ACH Routing/Account Number or Wire Instructions. We do not chase paper checks.
Payment Terms Define the strict due date logic (e.g., Net 15, Net 30, Due on Receipt).
03 Communication Channels
Dedicated Billing Email The exact email address where invoices are sent. This connects to our OCR "Gatekeeper" software.
Primary Point of Contact Name, Email, and Phone of the account representative for dispute resolution.
The Golden Rule
"No W-9, No Pay." This policy is absolute. No funds will be released to a vendor until their tax identification status is verified and stored in the General Ledger.
